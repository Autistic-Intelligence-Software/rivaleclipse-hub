FAQs

Account & sign-in

I didn't get my magic-link email.

Check spam/promotions. Links expire after 15 minutes — request a fresh one on the sign-in page. If it keeps failing, sign in with Discord instead.

My passkey isn't offered on this device.

Passkeys live on the device where you created them. Sign in with a magic link or Discord, then add a passkey on the new device from your dashboard.

Do I need to pair Discord?

Only for bot dashboards (Bouncer, Chat-Mate, Tim, VibeCheck, JayRain, Voice-Cord, Board-Cord) — they need your Discord identity to know which servers are yours. Requesting services, the client portal, web apps, and everything else works with just your email. You can link Discord anytime from your dashboards page.

I signed in but a dashboard asks me to sign in again.

Make sure you're on a rivaleclipse.dev address — the shared login only works across our own domains. If it persists, sign out and back in once.

Bots & dashboards

The bot isn't in my server / commands don't show.

Use the Add to Server button on the Discord Bots page. Slash commands can take up to an hour to appear the first time.

My dashboard doesn't list my server.

Dashboards list servers where your Discord account has Manage Server permission. Check you're logged into the right Discord account.

Something on a dashboard looks broken.

Hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). If it still misbehaves, tell us which dashboard and what you clicked — screenshots help. Use the contact route below.

Billing

How do subscriptions work across apps?

Each app bills separately — you can try, buy, or cancel any one without affecting the others. Bundle packages are the exception: one payment covering several apps.

How do I cancel?

From your client dashboard → subscriptions. Cancelling one app never touches another.

I was charged for something I cancelled.

Contact us with the app name and date — we'll sort it out.

Support

Getting support

Fastest first answers come from the site assistant — the chat button on every page knows the whole site and answers instantly, day or night. For anything it can't solve, or anything account-specific, send the form below — it files a support ticket with the team, and you can follow the conversation under My requests on your profile. Signed-in clients can also message the team directly from the client dashboard.

Feedback

Send feedback

Ideas, complaints, feature requests, "this page confused me" — we read all of it, and a lot of what's on this site exists because someone sent feedback. It lands with the dev team as a feedback ticket.

Contact Us

🔴 Live Chat Request

Want a human right now? Request a live chat — the team gets pinged immediately. If someone is free they'll claim it and the chat opens right here. If nobody is available, we'll ask you to leave the details as a ticket instead so nothing gets lost.

Waiting…

Contact us

Prefer to write it out? Describe what's happening and where, and be as specific as you can (which bot or page, what you clicked, what you expected). The more specific, the faster we fix it. Want something built instead? The dev request forms get you a fixed-price quote.
