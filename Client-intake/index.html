Intake FAQ

Do I pay anything to request a quote?

No. Quotes are free and carry no commitment.

Do I need an account to request?

Yes — you need to sign in before submitting a request, and each account can send one request per week. It takes a minute to create one, and it's where you'll track the project, approve milestones, and handle billing.

Who owns the finished work?

You do — code, designs, domains, and accounts, 100%.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Small fixes are covered; bigger changes get their own fixed quote. Ongoing care plans are available per app.
